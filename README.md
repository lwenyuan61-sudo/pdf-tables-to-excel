# PDF & image tables to Excel

Free tool that turns the tables in PDFs, scanned PDFs, photos and screenshots into an Excel file (.xlsx) or CSV.

**Use it:** https://lwenyuan61-sudo.github.io/pdf-tables-to-excel/

- Normal PDFs, scanned PDFs, PNG / JPG images — or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.
- Tables drawn with lines keep their structure: merged cells become merged cells in Excel, and text that wraps inside a cell stays in one cell.
- Built-in text recognition: English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish.
- Runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
- Keeps numbers as real numbers (thousands separators, currency, percentages, negatives in brackets), and keeps codes like `00123` as text.
- One sheet per page, or all pages in one sheet.

Text recognition can misread a character, especially in small screenshots of Chinese or Japanese text — zoom in before taking the screenshot, and check the numbers.

Built with [pdf.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/), [SheetJS](https://sheetjs.com/) and [Tesseract.js](https://tesseract.projectnaptha.com/).
